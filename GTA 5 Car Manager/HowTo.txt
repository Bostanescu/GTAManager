1. CE ESTE SI UNDE SE SALVEAZA DATELE
------------------------------------------------------------
Pagina tine evidenta masinilor din GTA Online: in ce garaj
sunt, pe ce loc, ce modificari au si cate locuri libere mai
ai in fiecare garaj.

IMPORTANT: datele NU se salveaza pe GitHub. Se salveaza doar
in browserul in care lucrezi (baza de date IndexedDB).
  - Alt calculator, alt browser sau telefon = pagina goala.
  - Daca stergi datele browserului (cache, cookies, "site
    data"), se sterg si masinile.
  - Modul incognito nu pastreaza nimic dupa inchidere.
Solutia: fa periodic export (sectiunea 11) si pastreaza
fisierele Cars.json si Garages.json.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. BARA DE SUS (HEADER)
------------------------------------------------------------
In mijloc: titlul "GTA Garage Manager". Fontul si culoarea
lui se schimba odata cu tema aleasa.

In dreapta, de la stanga la dreapta:
  ◐  Cercul colorat ... alegerea temei (sectiunea 5)
  EN / RO ............. limba paginii (sectiunea 4)
  🅿️ Garages/Garaje ... administrarea garajelor (sectiunea 10)
  ⋮  Meniul cu 3 puncte  import / export (sectiunea 11)

Meniurile se inchid cu click in afara lor sau cu tasta Esc.
Pe telefon, butoanele apar centrat sub titlu.

Daca apare un mesaj rosu "DB error" in bara de sus,
browserul blocheaza stocarea locala (vezi sectiunea 13).
Cand totul e in regula, nu apare nimic.

9. CAUTARE, FILTRE SI SORTARE
------------------------------------------------------------
CAUTARE (campul de sus) - cauta in: producator, model,
clasa, garaj, loc, note si tip Imani.
  - Nu conteaza diacriticele sau literele mari:
    "placuta" gaseste "plăcuță", "ubermacht" gaseste
    "Übermacht".

FILTRE CU BIFA (Favorite, Imani, Modified, HSW, Benny's)
  - Fiecare are iconita lui si, alaturi, numarul de masini
    care au optiunea respectiva.
  - Poti bifa mai multe deodata: se afiseaza doar masinile
    care le au pe TOATE.

LISTE DE FILTRARE
  - Garaj: arata si ocuparea, ex. "Agency (18/21)".
    Cand alegi un garaj, caseta de sus arata
    Ocupate / Libere / Total pentru el.
  - Clasa si Producator.

SORTARE
  - Originala (ordinea adaugarii), Clasa A→Z, Garaj A→Z,
    Favorite primele, Modificate primele.

"Results: X / Y" arata cate masini se vad din total.


------------------------------------------------------------
10. GARAJE SI LOCURI OCUPATE
------------------------------------------------------------
Butonul "🅿️ Garages" / "Garaje" (dreapta sus) deschide
administrarea garajelor.
  - Adaugare: scrii numele si numarul de locuri, apoi
    "Save garage".
  - Editare (✏️) si stergere (🗑️) din tabel. Stergerea unui
    garaj NU sterge masinile din el, doar definitia locurilor.
  - Garajele folosite la masini dar nedefinite apar in tabel
    cu "(undefined)" si un buton ➕ pentru definire rapida.

ATENTIE: numele garajului trebuie scris EXACT la fel la
masini si in lista de garaje (aceleasi litere, spatii,
majuscule), altfel ocuparea nu se calculeaza corect.
Cel mai sigur: alege garajul din lista care apare cand scrii.

GARAJ PLIN
  - In formularul masinii, sub campul Garaj:
      verde = mai sunt locuri libere
      rosu "⚠ Garage full" = nu mai sunt locuri
  - La salvarea intr-un garaj plin, pagina cere confirmare.
    Poti renunta sau salva oricum.
  - Daca editezi o masina care e deja in garajul respectiv,
    nu primesti avertismentul.

11. IMPORT SI EXPORT (BACKUP)
------------------------------------------------------------
Toate se afla in meniul ⋮ (trei puncte, dreapta sus):

  Cars / Masini
    📤 Export Cars.json ..... descarca toate masinile,
                              CU POZE
    📥 Import Cars.json ..... incarca masinile dintr-un fisier
  Garages / Garaje
    📤 Export Garages.json .. descarca garajele cu locurile
    📥 Import Garages.json .. incarca garajele dintr-un fisier

!!! ATENTIE: IMPORTUL INLOCUIESTE TOT !!!
Importul de masini sterge toate masinile existente si le
pune pe cele din fisier. La fel si importul de garaje.
Inainte de orice import, fa un export ca backup.

Recomandare: fa export dupa fiecare sesiune in care ai
modificat ceva si pastreaza fisierele intr-un loc sigur
(ex. Google Drive). Poti pastra mai multe versiuni
redenumite cu data, ex. "Cars_2026-10-17.json". La import,
numele fisierului nu conteaza.

13. PROBLEME FRECVENTE
------------------------------------------------------------
Pagina e goala
  -> Esti pe alt browser/dispozitiv sau s-au sters datele
     browserului. Din meniul ⋮: "Import Cars.json" si
     "Import Garages.json".

Nu vad ultimele modificari dupa ce am urcat fisierul pe
GitHub
  -> GitHub Pages are nevoie de 1-2 minute sa se actualizeze.
     Apoi apasa Ctrl+F5 ca sa ocolesti cache-ul.

Link-ul din index.html nu deschide pagina
  -> Fisierul trebuie sa se numeasca exact
     "GTA_Car_Manager.html", in folderul "GTA 5 Car Manager".

Ocuparea unui garaj apare gresita sau "?"
  -> Numele garajului de la masini difera de cel din lista
     de garaje, sau garajul nu are numar de locuri definit
     (sectiunea 10).

Pozele arata sterse
  -> Au fost salvate cu o versiune mai veche a paginii.
     Editeaza masina (✏️) si incarca din nou poza originala.

Titlul apare cu un font obisnuit
  -> Fonturile vin de pe Google Fonts; verifica conexiunea
     la internet sau blocantele de reclame/scripturi.

Apare "DB error" in bara de sus
  -> Browserul blocheaza stocarea locala (setari de
     confidentialitate, incognito). Deschide pagina normal.

1. WHAT IT IS AND WHERE DATA IS STORED
------------------------------------------------------------
The page keeps track of your GTA Online cars: which garage
they're in, which spot, what upgrades they have and how many
free slots each garage has left.

IMPORTANT: your data is NOT stored on GitHub. It is stored
only in the browser you are using (IndexedDB database).
  - Another computer, another browser or a phone = empty page.
  - Clearing browser data (cache, cookies, "site data")
    also deletes your cars.
  - Incognito mode keeps nothing after you close it.
Solution: export regularly (section 11) and keep the
Cars.json and Garages.json files.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. TOP BAR (HEADER)
------------------------------------------------------------
Center: the "GTA Garage Manager" title. Its font and color
change with the selected theme.

On the right, from left to right:
  ◐  Color circle ..... theme picker (section 5)
  EN / RO ............. page language (section 4)
  🅿️ Garages .......... garage management (section 10)
  ⋮  Three-dot menu ... import / export (section 11)

Menus close when you click outside them or press Esc.
On phones, the buttons are centered below the title.

If a red "DB error" message appears in the top bar, the
browser is blocking local storage (see section 13).
When everything works, nothing is shown.


------------------------------------------------------------
3. FIRST START
------------------------------------------------------------
On a new device the page is empty. Two options:
  a) Import your backups from the ⋮ menu:
     "Import Cars.json" and "Import Garages.json".
  b) Start from scratch: define your garages (section 10),
     then add your cars (section 6).


------------------------------------------------------------
4. PAGE LANGUAGE (EN / RO)
------------------------------------------------------------
The small EN / RO menu at the top right switches language.
  - The first time it opens, the page is in English.
  - Once you pick RO, the page stays in Romanian on later
    visits (in the same browser).
In-game names (classes, garages, Imani, HSW, Benny's) and
theme names stay the same in both languages.


------------------------------------------------------------
5. COLOR THEMES
------------------------------------------------------------
Click the color circle at the top right.

READY-MADE THEMES (12)
  Dark:   Graphite (default), Midnight, OLED Black,
          Los Santos Green, Neon Night, Vice Sunset,
          Ocean, Crimson, Mocha
  Light:  Light, Paper, Desert
Each theme changes everything: background, cards, text,
buttons, status colors and the title font.
All of them were checked for easy-to-read text.

CUSTOM THEME
  - Background: the page color.
  - Accent: the color of buttons and active filters.
  - Title font: the title's font (12 options).
  - Changes apply instantly.
  - The text color is picked automatically (light or dark)
    so it stays readable on any background. If you pick a
    "middle" color (e.g. bright magenta), the page makes it
    slightly lighter or darker while keeping the hue.

The selected theme is saved in the browser and kept on later
visits. On another browser/device it starts with Graphite.

Note: title fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Without an
internet connection the title uses a regular font.

6. ADDING A CAR
------------------------------------------------------------
Press "Add". The fields:
  - Manufacturer ............... e.g. Pegassi
  - Model ...................... e.g. Oppressor MK II
  - Class / Type ............... e.g. Super, Muscle, Off-Road

  - Image ...................... a screenshot of the car

9. SEARCH, FILTERS AND SORTING
------------------------------------------------------------
SEARCH (top field) - looks in: manufacturer, model, class,
garage, spot, notes and Imani type.
  - Accents and letter case don't matter:
    "ubermacht" finds "Übermacht".

CHECKBOX FILTERS (Favorite, Imani, Modified, HSW, Benny's)
  - Each one shows its icon and, next to it, how many cars
    have that option.
  - You can tick several at once: only cars that have ALL
    of them are shown.

DROPDOWN FILTERS
  - Garage: also shows occupancy, e.g. "Agency (18/21)".
    When you pick a garage, the box at the top shows
    Occupied / Free / Total for it.
  - Class and Manufacturer.

SORTING
  - Original (order added), Class A→Z, Garage A→Z,
    Favorites first, Modified first.

"Results: X / Y" shows how many cars are visible out of the
total.


------------------------------------------------------------
10. GARAGES AND OCCUPIED SLOTS
------------------------------------------------------------
The "🅿️ Garages" button (top right) opens garage management.
  - Add: type the name and number of slots, then
    "Save garage".
  - Edit (✏️) and delete (🗑️) from the table. Deleting a
    garage does NOT delete its cars, only the slot definition.
  - Garages used on cars but not defined show up in the
    table as "(undefined)" with a ➕ button for quick setup.

WARNING: the garage name must be written EXACTLY the same on
cars and in the garage list (same letters, spaces, case),
otherwise occupancy is calculated wrong.
Safest: pick the garage from the list that appears as you
type.

FULL GARAGE
  - In the car form, below the Garage field:
      green = free slots available
      red "⚠ Garage full" = no free slots
  - When saving into a full garage, the page asks you to
    confirm. You can cancel or save anyway.
  - If you edit a car that is already in that garage, you
    don't get the warning.


------------------------------------------------------------
11. IMPORT AND EXPORT (BACKUP)
------------------------------------------------------------
Everything is in the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right):

  Cars
    📤 Export Cars.json ..... downloads all cars,
                              WITH IMAGES
    📥 Import Cars.json ..... loads cars from a file
  Garages
    📤 Export Garages.json .. downloads garages with slots
    📥 Import Garages.json .. loads garages from a file

!!! WARNING: IMPORT REPLACES EVERYTHING !!!
Importing cars deletes all existing cars and replaces them
with the ones in the file. Importing garages works the same.
Before any import, export a backup first.

Tip: export after every session in which you changed
something and keep the files somewhere safe (e.g. Google
Drive). You can keep several versions renamed with the date,
e.g. "Cars_2026-10-17.json". The file name doesn't matter
when importing.


------------------------------------------------------------
12. IMAGES
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Use original screenshots, as large as possible.
  - The page automatically shrinks them to 1000 px wide and
    saves them in good quality (WebP). Smaller images are
    not artificially enlarged.
  - Images are included in Cars.json, so the file can reach
    a few MB. That's normal.
  - On the card, the image is cropped to fill the frame;
    when enlarged, all images have the same size.

13. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------
The page is empty
  -> You're on another browser/device, or browser data was
     cleared. From the ⋮ menu: "Import Cars.json" and
     "Import Garages.json".

I don't see my latest changes after uploading to GitHub
  -> GitHub Pages needs 1-2 minutes to update. Then press
     Ctrl+F5 to bypass the cache.

The link in index.html doesn't open the page
  -> The file must be named exactly "GTA_Car_Manager.html",
     inside the "GTA 5 Car Manager" folder.

A garage's occupancy looks wrong or shows "?"
  -> The garage name on the cars differs from the one in the
     garage list, or the garage has no slot count defined
     (section 10).

Images look blurry
  -> They were saved with an older version of the page.
     Edit the car (✏️) and upload the original image again.

The title shows a regular font
  -> Fonts come from Google Fonts; check your internet
     connection or ad/script blockers.

"DB error" appears in the top bar
  -> The browser is blocking local storage (privacy
     settings, incognito). Open the page normally.
